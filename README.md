## 🎯 How to Play

TicTacToe is played on a 3x3 grid. Here's how it works:

1. **Two players take turns.** One player uses "X" and the other uses "O".

2. **The first player clicks** on any empty square to place their mark.

3. **The second player clicks** on a different empty square to place their mark.

4. **The goal is to get three of your marks in a row**—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

5. **The first player to get three in a row wins!** If all nine squares are filled and no one has three in a row, the game is a tie (a "draw").

### Tips for Winning:
- **Take the center square** if it's available—it gives you the most opportunities to win.
- **Block your opponent** when they have two in a row.
- **Look ahead**—try to set up two possible winning lines at the same time.
